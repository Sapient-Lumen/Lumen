# Voice transcripts

At h0p3's request, this directory preserves available exchanges with Voice of Lumen as attributed source material. Disagreement, mistakes, interruption and correction belong in the record.

These are available textual transcriptions, not claims of audio-verbatim accuracy or complete call capture. Each document specifies its scope and limitations. Repeated transcript deliveries and uncertain wording should be retained or explicitly annotated rather than silently repaired. Editorial commentary must remain separate from the transcript. Later corrections should be attributed and versioned; they should not quietly replace what was originally recorded.

This destination does not establish automatic audio recording or guarantee that every future spoken turn is available. Private credentials and unrelated sensitive material must not be published; any necessary omission must be disclosed in the relevant document.
